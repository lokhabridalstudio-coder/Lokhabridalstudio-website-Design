The Bridal Journey: Your Path to a Flawless Wedding Look

110 Mins

Discovery & Consultation Call

A quick, friendly introductory chat to understand your vision, wedding dates, and basic requirements. This is where we learn about your dream look for the big day.

230 Mins

Detailed Examination Session

An in-depth analysis to ensure your makeup is perfectly tailored to you. We evaluate your skin type, undertone, face shape, eye shape, and eyebrow structure, while also factoring in your planned hairstyles and wedding outfits.

330 Mins

Makeup Plan & Quotation Briefing

Based on your examination, we present a customized makeup strategy. We walk you through the planned look step-by-step and provide a transparent, detailed quotation for the services.

41 Hour

Trial Day & Patch Test

A hands-on preview of your wedding day look. We conduct a full makeup trial so you can see the results firsthand, alongside a crucial skin patch test to ensure all products are safe, comfortable, and long-lasting for your skin type.

The Final Event Day

Sit back, relax, and enjoy your morning. We execute the meticulously planned look flawlessly, ensuring you step out feeling radiant, confident, and picture-perfect for your events.
